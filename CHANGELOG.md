# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-25)

- `nirutoday --brief`: today as one line of JSON (date, week, weather now and
  the day's high and low, warnings, VMA, name day, sunrise and sunset, the
  price now and today's events) from the cache only, without the network.
  NIRUPANEL's Today module shows it.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-24)

- First release: weather from SMHI, news from Sveriges Radio (P4 Väst and
  Ekot, local news first), VMA, SMHI warnings and Krisinformation, the
  electricity price per hour, name day, sunrise and sunset, today's calendar
  events and optional traffic from Trafikverket, in a teletext look that
  follows the NIRU theme.
- Listen plays P4 Väst live or Ekot's newest broadcast in NIRUAMP.
- Notices for a new VMA or an orange or red warning, once each.
- `nirutoday --print` for a terminal.
